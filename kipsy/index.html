Kipsy

Repeat what you watch, line by line.

  • Record while you watch. A small floating button starts an audio or screen recording over any app.
  • Subtitles made on your phone. Speech is turned into lines on the device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Repeat any line. Tap a line to hear it again, hold it to loop it, slow it down, and star the ones you want to keep.

Free and Premium

Every feature is free, with up to 10 recordings kept. Premium only removes that limit. It is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, through Google Play.
